Marikina City, Philippines

Marikina City is a highly urbanized city located in the eastern part of Metro Manila, Philippines. Known as the “Shoe Capital of the Philippines,” it is famous for its long history of shoe-making and for producing a large portion of the country’s locally made footwear. The city is also recognized for its clean streets, organized urban planning, bike-friendly roads, and strong sense of community. Marikina features attractions such as the Marikina River Park and the Shoe Museum, while balancing modern commercial areas with a peaceful residential atmosphere. Many people also describe Marikina as one of the cleanest and most livable cities in Metro Manila because of its disciplined traffic system, pedestrian-friendly sidewalks, and green public spaces.
