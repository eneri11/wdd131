Travel Tips

Make the most of your trip to the Philippines with these helpful travel tips.

Before You Travel

  • Travel during the dry season (November to May) for the best weather.
  • Book accommodations and flights in advance during peak seasons.
  • Carry sunscreen, a hat, and light clothing for tropical conditions.

During Your Trip

  • Stay hydrated, especially when visiting beaches and outdoor attractions.
  • Keep your valuables secure in crowded areas.
  • Respect local customs and cultural traditions.

Transportation Tips

  • Use jeepneys, buses, and tricycles for affordable local transportation.
  • Consider domestic flights when traveling between islands.
  • Download maps and travel apps before your trip.
