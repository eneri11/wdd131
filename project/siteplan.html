Explore Philippines Site Plan

Travel and Tourism Website Project

Site Name

Explore Philippines

The name "Explore Philippines" was selected because it clearly represents the website's purpose of helping visitors discover tourist destinations, cultural attractions, and travel experiences throughout the Philippines.

Optional Domain: explorephilippines.org

Site Purpose

Explore Philippines is a travel and tourism website that provides information about tourist destinations, cultural attractions, travel guides, travel tips, and visitor resources. The website helps users discover places to visit and plan future trips.

Scenarios

  • What are the best beach destinations to visit in the Philippines?
  • What travel tips should I know before visiting the Philippines?
  • Which destinations are best for family vacations?
  • How can I contact the website for travel recommendations?

Color Schema

#005F73
#0A9396
  • #005F73 (Deep Blue) – Used for headings, navigation, footer, and branding.
  • #0A9396 (Teal) – Used for buttons, links, accents, and interactive elements.
  • #FFFFFF (White) – Used for content backgrounds.
  • #F4F4F4 (Light Gray) – Used for page background areas.

Typography

  • Poppins – Used for headings (H1, H2, H3).
  • Open Sans – Used for paragraphs, lists, forms, and body text.
